# Longer six-week fieldwork history (exploratory)

The Kaggle follow-up notebook version 11 compares the existing six-week fieldwork model with an otherwise identical model trained on additional complete earlier years. Re-run all notebook cells to reproduce; the input snapshots, coverage details, predictions and results remain on Kaggle. The code is in analysis_longer_fieldwork_history.py and depends on the notebook's earlier cells and input snapshots. No raw archive downloads are retained.

The comparison holds the evaluation years, price-only baseline, forecasting cutoff, features, regularization and training-only standardization constant. It requires the old-model results to reproduce the existing predictions before comparison, and uses only revenue filings dated before each evaluation cutoff. Additional years 2012, 2014 and 2015 have complete six-week, forecast and price records; 2010, 2011 and 2013 are excluded because fieldwork records are incomplete. A 2013 source also has inconsistent text and table values.

Historical prices are reconstructed from original BLS April releases and the prior April's four-month-revised index published in September, as in [April 2014](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/ppi_05142014.htm) and [September 2013](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/ppi_09132013.htm). These are ratios of published rounded index values, not ALFRED vintage extracts. Forecast data are compact summaries of [Iowa Environmental Mesonet MOS archives](https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/mos/); the archived summaries may have been revised and their historical publication timing has not been independently verified. Forecast systems also change over time; see the [NOAA model change notice](https://www.weather.gov/media/notification/tins/tin12-42land_surface_model.pdf). Fieldwork records include attributed contemporary reproductions or later reports' prior-week values, which may incorporate revisions. Exact dates, links and transcription details are retained in the notebook. Code checks metadata and arithmetic but cannot verify transcription against the sources.

All evaluation years through 2026 were previously examined. The finding is exploratory and does not establish a reliable improvement, a causal relationship, or an untouched holdout. CF revenue is company-wide, not Iowa corn sales. The frozen prospective 2027 protocol is unchanged.
