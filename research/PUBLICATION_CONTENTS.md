# Publication snapshot

This source snapshot includes the saved research notebook and selected results in `iowa-fertilizer-revenue-price-follow-up.ipynb` and `research-results.zip`. Files ending in `_private` retain their original names from the earlier workflow; those files are included in this publication snapshot. Bytecode caches and duplicate copies of source files from notebook output have been excluded.

The notebook preserves numerical outputs from Kaggle version 48 and its successful 196-test run. Source comments and descriptive labels have been edited for publication. It is an observational record of a completed run, not a standalone reproducible bundle: its Kaggle metadata references a separate dataset version and a previous notebook version, and some cells rely on their mounted outputs. Those source resources are not included in full and remain separately access controlled. The exported notebook also retains its saved outputs.

The results are historical and exploratory. Historical weather forecast availability and archive revision history remain unverified. The 2027 protocol was fixed after earlier research; it has not been prospectively tested. A checksum identifies captured content but does not establish when it was publicly available.

Source excerpts include public U.S. government agency contact information from the original Bureau of Labor Statistics releases; they were preserved as source evidence. There are no credentials intentionally included. Publication does not make the separately stored Kaggle dataset or notebook public.
