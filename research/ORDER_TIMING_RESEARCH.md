# Fertilizer buying, forecasts and reorders

Research note; not a new input to the fixed 2027 forecast.

- USDA says corn growers in the U.S. buy fertilizer in the months before most April–May planting; this is not an Iowa monthly order distribution. [USDA ERS](https://ers.usda.gov/data-products/charts-of-note/108828)
- Farmers can use forecasts in their fieldwork decisions. Iowa State discusses 6–10 day forecasts for timing **fall nitrogen application**; that is not evidence about buying after a rain-app alert or spring order frequency. [Iowa State](https://crops.extension.iastate.edu/post/sustained-soil-temperatures-below-50-degrees-needed-fall-nitrogen-application)
- No trustworthy public average transaction size for Iowa corn farmers or dealer reorder threshold was identified. Farm annual application rates, truck capacities and sales divided by customers are not the average order size. Iowa State reports an average of 170–183 lb of commercial nitrogen per corn acre in corn–soybean rotations for 2017–2023; this is nitrogen applied over a season, not a purchase or a rain-triggered amount. [Iowa State retailer survey](https://nrstracking.cals.iastate.edu/accessibility-version-tracking-iowa-nutrient-reduction-strategy-v4)
- Illustrative calculation only: Iowa State suggested 30–60 lb of nitrogen per acre for justified late-season 2025 rescue applications. For 100 affected acres that would be 3,000–6,000 lb of nitrogen, or 1.5–3 U.S. short tons of nutrient. This is not a measured order size, does not imply a purchase or immediate CF sale, and concerns July 2025 (after the Q2 window). [Iowa State](https://crops.extension.iastate.edu/post/rescue-nitrogen-corn-iowa-it-too-late-2025)
- Dealers may buy and hold inventory ahead of farmer purchases. A later farmer purchase may use existing stock; the dealer might reorder later, from another supplier, or not within the quarter. No universal delay or reorder quantity was established. [Oklahoma State](https://extension.okstate.edu/fact-sheets/managing-fertilizer-price-risk); [CF annual report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1324404/000132440426000007/cf-20251231.htm)
- [Iowa State’s historical weather-forecast archive](https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/mos/) may support later research into forecasts available before the May 31 cutoff. Check station coverage, precipitation variables, issue time, and archive reliability before use. Forecasts are not evidence that a farmer saw them or placed an order. Never substitute subsequently observed rain for a forecast issued beforehand.

Keep the original 2027 forecasts frozen. A separate future experiment would require a pre-specified forecast feature and honest time-ordered comparison, with no unseen holdout claim for already-inspected 2012–2026 years. Raw forecast downloads remain on Kaggle; retain only small aggregates and source metadata.
