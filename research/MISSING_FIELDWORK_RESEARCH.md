# Recovered Iowa fieldwork weeks: retrospective comparison

The Kaggle follow-up notebook version 12 compares adding the missing 2010, 2011 and 2013 training years to the previous longer-history model. It uses identical 2022–2026 prediction folds, features, training-only scaling and penalty; the earlier predictions must reproduce exactly. Analysis code is in analysis_missing_fieldwork.py; run it after the existing notebook cells and compact inputs. The compact source data and results stay on Kaggle.

The [USDA state stories for May 17, 2011](https://esmis.nal.usda.gov/sites/default/release-files/fq977t77k/br86b599x/tx31qk76m/WWStateStories-05-17-2011.pdf) reports the missing 2011 May 15 week. [USDA state stories for May 7, 2013](https://esmis.nal.usda.gov/sites/default/release-files/fq977t77k/2f75rb11r/707959814/WWStateStories-05-07-2013.pdf) and the narrative of the original [Iowa May 6 report](https://publications.iowa.gov/14585/1/Vol08__05_06_13.pdf) agree on the May 5 value, but the same Iowa report table differs. Both values are tested as a sensitivity.

The remaining four missing weeks are reconstructed by differencing consecutive observations in the current [Iowa Environmental Mesonet statewide NASS series](https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/plotting/auto/plot/127/state:IA::short_desc:FD::w:last::threshold:50::cmap:tab20b::_r:43::dpi:100.csv). The source is a current, potentially revised archive; it does not establish the values available at the original prediction cutoff. Retain retrieval date and provenance; do not attribute a contemporary report date to these reconstructions. The prior study's forecast-vintage and price-reconstruction caveats also apply. The test is retrospective and all test years were previously inspected. It does not change the frozen 2027 protocol.
